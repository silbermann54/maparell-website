Good to know

Where do my workouts come from?

From Apple Health. Every workout with a GPS route lands on your map, for example the ones you record with Apple Watch. New workouts come in automatically.

Is Maparell free?

Yes. The map, the diary, summits, photos and workout details are free, with ads. Maparell Pro adds statistics, satellite maps, GPX import and heart-rate zones and removes the ads. You can choose a yearly plan with one week free, a monthly plan or a one-time purchase.

Can I import GPX files?

Yes, with Maparell Pro. Imported tours appear on the map like any other route.

Where can I get Maparell?

On the App Store in the EU, Norway, Iceland, Switzerland and the UK, for iPhone and iPad.
